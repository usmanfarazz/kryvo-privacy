## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Bijli Kab? tells you when the light will go — before it goes.

Your neighbours tap "Light Gayi" or "Light Aayi". Bijli Kab? combines the reports
from your area, shows the status live, and learns your area's loadshedding
pattern to warn you before the next cut.

⚡ ONE-TAP REPORTS
• Light gone? Light back? Tell your whole street in one second
• Add a detail: low voltage, tripping, transformer fault, scheduled cut
• Smart voting: one wrong tap is out-voted by the neighbours

🔮 FORECAST
• Predicts the next cut and how long it will last
• Tells you when the light is likely to come back
• Weekly heat map: see when cuts usually happen
• Add your electricity company's official schedule

🔔 ALERTS
• Warning 5 to 60 minutes before a likely cut
• "Light is back!" alerts
• Before-the-cut checklist: charge the phone, fill the water tank, iron the clothes
• Quiet hours at night

🗺️ LIVE MAP
• See which nearby areas have light right now
• Follow your home, office, school and family's areas

📊 STATS
• Hours without light this week and month
• Longest and average cut, worst hours of the day
• Share a beautiful stats card on WhatsApp

🏆 POINTS & BADGES
• Earn points for every report, extra for being first
• 7 levels from Newcomer to Bijli Legend, 16 badges, streaks
• Leaderboard: everyone or your city

🔋 POWER TOOLS
• UPS backup calculator — will it last through the next cut?
• Solar planner: panels, inverter and battery size
• Electricity bill estimate

🎯 FUN & GAMES
• Andaza Lagao: guess when the light comes back and win points
• Confetti when the light comes back — with chime, bell, siren or horn alerts
• Bijli Wrapped: your last 4 weeks as shareable story slides
• Weekly challenges, area ranking and area mood reactions

🛠️ HANDY
• One-tap complaint call / SMS to your electricity helpline
• UPS "running on battery" and "fully charged" reminders
• Water pump timer: run it when the light comes back, stop when the tank is full
• Invite friends with your area code
• Optional App Lock (off by default — no password needed)

📱 HOME-SCREEN WIDGET
See your area's status without opening the app.

🎨 12 THEMES · 🌐 ROMAN URDU, اردو, हिन्दी, ENGLISH

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No phone number, e-mail or real name needed. Your exact location never leaves
the phone — only the ~1 km area code is sent with a report.

Forecasts are estimates based on community reports and are not official
information from any electricity company.
```
