⚡ Bijli Kab? — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026 · Developer: Faraz Labs · Contact: usmanfaraz1818@gmail.com

In short: no phone number, e-mail or real name is needed. Your exact location never leaves your phone. When you report that the light went or came back, we store that report with the code of your ~1 km area and a random anonymous ID, so your neighbours can see the status.

What stays on your phone

Your precise GPS position (used only to find your area), the areas you follow and their names, your settings, checklist, schedule, calculator inputs, guesses, challenge progress, complaint numbers and helpline, and — if you turn on App Lock — a salted hash of your PIN (never the PIN itself). Fingerprints are checked by Android and never reach the app. Uninstalling the app or Settings → Reset app deletes them.

What we don't do

No ads, no advertising IDs, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of data. We do not show other users who sent a report.

Services we use

Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and, if enabled, Cloud Messaging for push alerts) stores the data above on Google's servers; see Firebase privacy. OpenStreetMap map tiles are downloaded when you open the map; see the OSMF privacy policy. Your phone's built-in geocoder turns a location into a place name.

Permissions

Location — only while you choose an area, in the foreground. Notifications — outage warnings and "light is back" alerts. Run at startup — to restore scheduled warnings after a restart. Internet — to send and receive reports.

Your choices

You can use any nickname, turn off any alert, and reset the app at any time. To delete your leaderboard profile and reports linked to your anonymous ID, e-mail us from the app (More → How it works → Send feedback); we will do it within 30 days.

Children

Bijli Kab? is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Forecasts

Forecasts are estimates made from community reports. They are not official information from any electricity company.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
